## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Please read through the following FAQs before reaching out to the team.

#### Q1. How long will I have access to the shared folder/link?
- The shared link is valid for 48 hours from the time it is sent to you - NOT from the time youfirst open it. Please ensure you begin and complete your work well within this window. Do not wait until the last moment to start.

#### Q2. Can the deadline be extended?
- Link extensions will only be considered in exceptional and valid circumstances, at the sole discretion of the team. If you believe you have a genuine reason for requiring an extension, please write to us at: Team@intucate.com

- Note: Extension requests made without a valid reason will not be entertained.

#### Q3. Is submitting the case study mandatory for the interview process?
- Yes, absolutely. The case study submission is directly linked to your interview process. If the case study is not submitted within the given timeframe, your candidature will not be considered further and you will not be eligible to proceed to the next round of the interview.

#### Q4. I accidentally closed the link - does my 48-hour window reset?
- No. The 48-hour window begins from the time the link is shared with you, regardless of when you open or close it. Please plan your time accordingly. 

#### Q5. Is it mandatory to use the OpenAI API?
- No. The use of the OpenAI API is completely optional. You are free to use a mock implementation, a free alternative (such as open-source LLMs or local models), or any other approach you prefer. What matters most is your backend design, async handling, database structure, and overall code clarity - not the specific API used.

#### Q6. What tech stack should I use?
- There is no strict requirement on the tech stack. Use what you are most comfortable andproficient with. However, ensure your choices are well-justified - you may be asked to explain your decisions during the review discussion.

#### Q7. Will there be a code review or technical discussion after submission?   
- Yes. After reviewing your submission, the team will schedule a discussion where you will be asked to walk through your implementation decisions, architecture choices, and any trade- offs you made. Be prepared to explain your approach clearly.
